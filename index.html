Smart Accident & Disaster Alert System

Arduino-based IoT safety system built for two real-world hazard scenarios: blind mountain-curve vehicle collisions and flood risk from rising water levels. Ultrasonic sensors on each side of the road trigger a warning LED on the opposite side, while a water-level sensor triggers a buzzer as an early flood alert.

  • Arduino UNO
  • Ultrasonic Sensors
  • Embedded C
  • IoT
